Privacy policy

Last updated 17 November 2026.

WikiShorts Uploader is a small tool that uploads videos to the PepperYaps YouTube channel. Only the channel's owner uses it.

  • It uses the YouTube Data API to upload the owner's own videos and to read the channel's name.
  • The sign-in token is kept on the owner's own computer. It is not sent anywhere else.
  • It does not collect, store or share data about anyone else.

Its use of information from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. See also YouTube's Terms of Service and Google's Privacy Policy.

Questions: contact us through the PepperYaps channel.
